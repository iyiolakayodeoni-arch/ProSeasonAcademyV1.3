# The Pitch

Everything organised in this repo compresses into the versions below. Same claim in each, tuned to
who is listening.

---

## The one-liner

> **Onliversity is a university structure for creative kids in Africa — a real cohort, a real
> curriculum, and a season with a finish line. Built by the students, in the open.**

## The elevator version (30 seconds)

> A child who draws, writes, designs or films has been told their dream doesn't count. Not because
> it isn't valuable — because nobody built a structure for it. Universities work because they have
> four things: someone teaching, people learning alongside you, a curriculum with levels, and a
> credential at the end. Onliversity gives creative kids that structure, adapted for how creative
> work is actually learned — and it arrives in seasons, because we will not hand out a credential
> we have not earned. Season 1 is a solo journey. The AI coach lands in Season 2. The certificate
> unlocks only when ten of us have made it as pros. We are not asking anyone to value the sciences
> less. We are building the seat next to them that was never built. Online now, physical
> inshallah.

## The one-pager

**The problem.** A child with real creative ability learns early that it does not count. Nobody
enrols them, nobody examines them, nobody hands them anything at the end. The belief that creative
careers are not important is not really about value — it is about the absence of structure.

**The insight.** That belief was not built by argument. It was built by infrastructure. Which means
it cannot be defeated by argument either. You build the structure, and the belief moves.

**The solution.** The same structure a university has, better suited to creative careers:

| University | Onliversity | When |
|---|---|---|
| A professor | An AI coach that does the research and watches your matches | Season 2 |
| A class | A named cohort moving together | Season 1 |
| A syllabus | Levels, with the ladder and your matches as the assessment | Season 1 |
| Exams | VOD review and your results on the ladder | Season 1 |
| A transcript | A **public record** now — matches, results, rank, VODs, lessons | Season 1 |
| A degree | The certificate — **gated on ten of us making it as pros** | At ten pros |
| A campus community | A live community of creatives across Africa | Season 1 |
| A campus | On the roadmap. Inshallah. | Later |

**What we are not.** Not anti-school. Not a shortcut. Not competing with the sciences. And not
claiming anything we have not built — which is why Season 1 says plainly that it has no coach and
no certificate yet.

**The proof plan.** Two tracks. Structural parity — visible from day one, no future success
required. And scale — Onliversity becoming undeniably big across Africa, which is the evidence
that actually changes minds. We publish our numbers, including the bad ones.

**How we operate.** Transparent by default. The students are involved in the process and nothing
is hidden from them. This is their uni.

## Tuned versions

The audience is the teenager. The parent version exists only because a parent will eventually read
this page on their own — it is not something we go and hand them.

### For a student — the one that matters

> You are not a customer here. This is our uni — yours. The curriculum is out in the open, the
> decisions get published, and when we do not know something we say so. Season 1 is you on your own
> — no coach, and no certificate yet, because we will not hand you a piece of paper we have not
> earned. What you get instead is a squad that notices when you are missing, a ladder that shows
> exactly where you are, and a record of every match you played. The AI coach comes in Season 2,
> and it gets built out of what you learn in Season 1. The certificate unlocks when ten of us make
> it as pros — and you can watch that counter move. And when this becomes a real building,
> inshallah, you will have been part of building it.

### For a partner

> We are building the structure that creative education in Africa has never had — cohort-based,
> season-based, and assessed by the ladder and your own recorded work. Season 1 is solo; the AI
> coach arrives in Season 2, built from what Season 1 learns. The certificate does not exist until
> ten of our players have made it as pros and been certified elsewhere. We publish everything,
> including our completion rates and the gaps.

### For a parent — passive, not a target

> Your child is not being asked to choose between this and school, and we are not here to tell you
> the sciences don't matter — they do. We are saying your child's craft deserves the same treatment
> the sciences already get: a real teacher with a name, a group of students learning alongside
> them, a curriculum with levels, and something issued at the end that you can hold. Look at the
> curriculum. Look at who is teaching. And then look at what your child has made by the end of it.
> Judge it on that.

## What we never say

- That we are accredited, when we are not.
- That we replace school.
- That creative work matters *more* than the sciences — we say it matters *too*.
- That this is easy or fast.
- That there is a coach, in Season 1. There isn't.
- That you get a certificate. Not until ten of us make it as pros.
- Anything that requires a number we are not willing to publish.
