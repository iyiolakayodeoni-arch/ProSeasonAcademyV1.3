## 1. WHAT THE PRODUCT IS (say this accurately — do not invent features)

**One line:**
> ProSeasonAcademy helps an EA SPORTS FC console player turn ONE played match into ONE honest lesson they carry into the next match.

**The core loop (this is the whole product):**
> **PLAY → REVIEW → CARRY ONE LESSON FORWARD.**

**What the player actually does:**
1. Plays a real console match (EA SPORTS FC 26/27).
2. Opens the app and starts a **Match Review** (called a "Mirror Session").
3. Before the match: sets one **intention** (what they're focusing on) and logs starting composure.
4. At half-time and full-time: answers a few questions in their **own words** — how they feel, what's happening.
5. After full-time: saves the score (a ~15-second log to the **Match Vault** / receipt).
6. Marks the **key moments** themselves and reviews them in their own words.
7. Sees four versions of their own thinking side by side — BEFORE / HALF-TIME / FULL-TIME / AFTER REVIEW — and answers *"which version is closest to the evidence?"*
8. Ends by swearing **one lesson** into **The Thread**.
9. The next session opens by asking whether that lesson **held or broke**.

**Progress:** a single universal six-stage journey — SEE YOURSELF → CONTROL YOURSELF → READ THE GAME → BUILD DISCIPLINE → PERFORM UNDER PRESSURE → PROVE IT. Stages only advance when the **evidence** (real saved matches, honest notes) earns them. No fake percentages, no time-spent progress.

**The Baseline Week:** a 7-day gate. Five matches, one a day. After each match the player watches their own recording, names the moments they failed, and analyses them in their own words. Day 6 is reflection; day 7 seals their profile. The next day unlocks 30 minutes after the previous seals — nothing is forced, lateness is never punished.

**The Standard:** a benchmark journey beside yours — a composite of the best in the path. Direction, not a second task. "Your Journey is the evidence. The Standard is the benchmark."

**Coaches / seats:** you lock in ONE coach permanently (by design). Season One is capped at **1,000 seats** — when it's full, it's full. A paid programme after a 14-day trial.

## 2. WHAT THE PRODUCT IS **NOT** (hard rules — never position it this way)

- ❌ NOT a tips feed of "this mechanic works."
- ❌ NOT a social network for gamers.
- ❌ NOT a card / stat generator or dashboard.
- ❌ NOT an **AI coach that watches your match and tells you what you did wrong.**
- ❌ NOT a shortcut to becoming a pro.

**The defining idea:** *The Mirror does not think for you.* The machine records the evidence; **the player does the seeing.** The app preserves your own thinking and places your intentions beside the recording until **self-deception becomes hard to maintain.**

Supporting taglines you may use:

- **THE MIRROR DOES NOT THINK FOR YOU.**
- **YOUR JOURNEY IS THE EVIDENCE. THE STANDARD IS THE BENCHMARK.**
- **CARRY ONE LESSON.**

**The problem it solves (for the video's opening tension):**
Most players play a lot but learn little from the matches they lose. They remember the score, blame the game / lag / the opponent / luck, and move to the next match. They repeat the same behaviour and call it bad luck. ProSeasonAcademy gives them a simple repeatable practice that turns self-deception into evidence.

- Serious EA SPORTS FC console players (26/27).
- Players who say they want to be exceptional but behave casually — people who **blame the game**.
- Players who want their **behaviour to match their ambition**.
- Console-only (PS/Xbox) players who record their own matches.
- Tone: broadcast-esports, cinematic, premium, near-black + one neon green. Not childish, not corporate.

**The close (56–64s)**

- Caption: `SEE YOURSELF. DO THE WORK.` + `1,000 SEATS ONLY`

## 6. SHOT-BY-SHOT VISUAL / STYLE PLAN (for the generator)

**Overall look:**
- Cinematic, broadcast-esports, film-grain, slow push-ins.
- Near-black background (`#050a06`), one neon brand green (`#39FF6A`), a warm gold accent (`#f2c078`) sparingly.
- Mono/terminal-style labels, uppercase, wide letter-spacing. Glass cards with HUD borders. A faint arena grid, subtle aurora glow, rising particles.
- A glowing infinity (∞) crest — it's the signature mark.

**Key set pieces:**
1. **The Arena** — dark stadium, floodlight, a lone player.
2. **The Mirror** — the player's reflection; the reflection sometimes disagrees.
3. **The Recording** — gameplay footage rewinding, timeline markers.
4. **The Ledger/Receipts** — a journal filling with honest entries; "YOU CANNOT OUTRUN YOUR RECEIPTS."
5. **The Thread** — one glowing lesson line carried forward.
6. **The Road / The Standard** — a six-stage journey line beside a benchmark line.
7. **The Crest** — the infinity mark that glows and pulses.

**Motion language:** slow, deliberate, confident. No chaotic fast cuts for the philosophy; use quick cuts only for the "blame the game" montage.
