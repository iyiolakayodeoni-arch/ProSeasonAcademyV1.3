# 14 — The Seasons Model

Raw source: [`13 — Source: The Seasons Model`](./13-source-seasons.md).

This document revises the structure in [`02 — The University Blueprint`](./02-the-university-blueprint.md).
It does not contradict it — it **sequences** it.

---

## 1. What changed

Two of the four pillars are not available at launch, and that is deliberate:

| Pillar | Status in Season 1 |
|---|---|
| 1 — Someone is teaching it | **No coach.** The AI coach arrives in Season 2. |
| 2 — People are learning with them | Present. |
| 3 — There is a cert after | **No certificate.** It unlocks at ten pros. |
| 4 — There is a structure | Present. This is what Season 1 actually is. |

So Season 1 is not a reduced version of the blueprint. It is **a different stage of it**, with its
own honest shape.

## 2. Season 1 — the solo journey

> *"We need to get you to be the best you can be on your own first, and learn lessons, go on that
> journey."*

**What it is.** The player, their own work, their own lessons. Onliversity supplies the structure —
the season, the levels, the record, the community — but not a teacher.

**What it is for.** Three things at once:

1. **It builds the player.** Self-directed improvement against a real competitive ladder.
2. **It builds the evidence.** Every match, result and lesson is recorded. Season 1's output is a
   body of work, not a certificate.
3. **It builds the coach.** This is the important one. The lessons learned in Season 1 — what
   actually improved play, what wasted time, what the research should have covered — are the raw
   material the Season 2 AI coach is built from. **The players who struggle through Season 1 are
   the ones who design the Season 2 coach.**

That third point is principle 2 in [`04 — Principles`](./04-principles.md) made literal: *the
students are co-builders, not customers.* They are not just shaping the feature list. They are
generating the knowledge the product runs on.

**What Season 1 does NOT have.** A coach. A certificate. Both are named explicitly, in public,
before anyone enrolls. See section 5.

## 3. Season 2 — the AI coach

> *"An AI coach that would actually help you to actively be competing — doing the research you need
> to get done, and watching your matches, and helping you in your day to day playing as a pro
> player."*

Pillar 1 arrives here. Four defined jobs:

| Job | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Actively competing** | Not tips in the abstract. Guidance aimed at winning the match in front of you. |
| **Doing the research** | The opponent, the meta, the patches — the work a pro does off the pitch. |
| **Watching your matches** | VOD review. Specific, timestamped, about *your* play. |
| **Day to day** | Practice routine, form, rest, tilt. The unglamorous part that actually decides it. |

**Why an AI coach is the right answer and not a compromise:**

- It is available to every player at once, which a human coach in Africa at this price point is not.
- It is the honest answer to *"hand dey meet everybody."* A human pro coach is unaffordable at the
  scale this needs. An AI one is not.
- It can watch every match. A human coach watches the ones they have time for.
- It gets better as Season 1's lessons accumulate, which no hire ever does.

**Why it is not a shortcut around pillar 1.** The pillar says *someone is teaching it* — the point
is that the craft is being transmitted by something that actually has it, rather than the student
guessing alone. An AI coach that has absorbed a season of real lessons and can watch your specific
matches meets that bar. A playlist does not.

## 4. The certificate — gated on ten pros

> *"The cert is not going to be there yet. It's only there once we have helped at least ten people
> become pros and win certificates."*

**This is the strongest decision in the whole project and it should be said out loud in public.**

Read what it actually commits to: Onliversity will not issue a credential until it has produced ten
people who became professionals and were certified elsewhere — by a tournament, a team, an
organiser. The certificate is not a promise about our opinion of you. It is a record that our
structure demonstrably works.

Why it is stronger than issuing one now:

| Issuing now | Gating on ten pros |
|---|---|
| A certificate we printed, backed by our own say-so | A certificate backed by ten people who made it |
| Worth whatever people believe about us | Worth something a parent can verify |
| Can be faked, so it invites suspicion | Cannot be faked, because the ten pros are public |

It is also a perfect fit with [`03 — The Evidence Strategy`](./03-the-evidence-strategy.md):
*evidence, not argument.* The certificate is rung 4 of the ladder — outcomes — and it refuses to
exist before rung 4 is real.

**The consequence:** the "ten pros" number becomes a public counter. Every person who makes it
moves the whole community one step closer to having a credential. That is a shared goal, and a
shared goal is what turns a cohort into a community.

## 5. What we now say out loud

This is principle 6 — *we do not claim what we have not built* — applied to the two things we do
not have.

| We do NOT say | We say instead |
|---|---|
| "There is a coach." | "Season 1 is solo — that's the point. The AI coach arrives in Season 2, built from what Season 1 learns." |
| "You get a certificate." | "There's no certificate yet. It unlocks when ten of us make it as pros. Here's the counter." |
| "The full university structure, today." | "Here's the structure. Here's exactly which parts exist now and which ones arrive next." |

Publishing the gap is not a weakness. It is the only version of this that a teenager who has been
lied to by the internet before will believe.

## 6. What this means for the four pillars

The pillars in [`02 — The University Blueprint`](./02-the-university-blueprint.md) remain the
definition of what Onliversity is **for**. They are the end state, not the launch state.

Restated as a sequence:

| | Season 1 | Season 2 | At ten pros |
|---|---|---|---|
| Structure | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Community | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Someone teaching | ❌ solo by design | ✅ AI coach | ✅ |
| A credential | ❌ the record instead | ❌ | ✅ |

**The rule that keeps this honest:** every one of those ❌ has to be visible to the student before
they join, and each one has to have a named season attached to it. A gap with a date on it is a
roadmap. A gap without one is a lie.

## 7. What Season 1 has to deliver instead

If there is no coach and no certificate, Season 1 still has to be worth the teenager's time on its
own. It has to clear this bar without either:

- [ ] A real ladder with visible progression — you can always see where you are and what's next
- [ ] A record that accumulates — matches, results, rank, VODs, and the lessons
- [ ] A community that is actually active daily, since there is no coach carrying the room
- [ ] Lessons captured in a structured way — because they are the Season 2 training data, not a
      byproduct
- [ ] A public ten-pro counter that everyone can see moving
- [ ] A finish line for the season itself, so it ends and the record closes

That last item matters most. A season without an ending is just an app.

## Open on the seasons

- What exactly counts as "becoming a pro"? A signed team, prize money above a threshold, an
  organiser's certification? The gate is meaningless if the word is soft.
- Who certifies the ten? If it is us, the gate proves nothing. It has to be external.
- What is the record called, given it is explicitly not a certificate?
- How long is Season 1, and what ends it?
- What does a player get at the end of Season 1, in their hands?
- What specifically does the AI coach need from Season 1's data to work?
