# 02 — The University Blueprint

The selling point is that we have the same structure a university has, but better suited to
creative careers. This document is that claim, made precise — element by element.

Two rules for the table below:

1. **Nothing is hand-waved.** If a university has it, we say what ours looks like.
2. **Nothing is faked.** Where we genuinely cannot yet match it, we say so and put it on the
   roadmap. See *What we refuse to fake* at the bottom.

---

## 1. The parity table

| University has | What it is actually doing | Onliversity's version | Why ours suits creative work better |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Faculty** — a professor teaching the course | Transmits the craft; proves it can be taught | An **AI coach** that does the research, watches your matches and works with you day to day — **arrives in Season 2** | A creative skill is learned from something that still does it, and that can watch *your* specific work. A lecturer who left the field ten years ago cannot teach you the current field; a playlist cannot watch you play. |
| **A cohort** — a class moving together | Removes the loneliness; creates peers and pressure | A named cohort that enrols, works and graduates together | Isolation is the single biggest killer of young creatives. A cohort turns a private hobby into a shared programme. |
| **A curriculum** — levels and a syllabus | Turns a talent into a discipline with progression | A portfolio spine: every course produces a finished, public piece of work | A creative curriculum is not measured in topics covered. It is measured in work that exists and can be shown. |
| **Assessment** — exams and grading | Proves you did the work | Critique, review and a shipped portfolio | An exam hall measures recall under pressure. A critique measures the work in front of you. For a creative, the second one is the real test. |
| **The credential** — a degree certificate | Says "this happened, and here is the proof" | A **public record** now; the **certificate** only once ten of our players have made it as pros | A transcript lists courses you sat. A record shows what you actually did — and a credential we refuse to issue until it is earned is worth more than one we print. |
| **A community** — a campus, clubs, societies | Belonging; the reason people stay | A live community of creatives across Africa, not just classmates | Your classmates graduate and disappear. A continental community of people who get it does not. |
| **Studios and workshops** — labs, studios, rehearsal rooms | Where the work is actually made | Studios and critique sessions as the primary classroom | The lecture hall is not where a creative is trained. The studio is. So the studio is the centre of ours, not an annex. |
| **Progression** — year 1 → 2 → 3, entry requirements | Standards; a real path from beginner to professional | Levels based on the work, not the birth year | A 14-year-old with a strong portfolio should not be held back by their age, and a 17-year-old starting from zero should not be pushed. |
| **A physical campus** | Permanence; the proof it is real | **On the roadmap. Inshallah.** Physical once the online structure is proven at scale. | We are honest that this is a stage, not a launch feature — and the honesty is part of the credibility. |
| **Accreditation** | Formal recognition by the state | **We do not have it, and we do not claim it.** Pursued later, once we have scale. | See *What we refuse to fake.* |

## 2. The four pillars, made operational

The parity table is the full picture, but the whole thing rests on four things. If a version of
Onliversity is missing any one of them, it is not Onliversity — it is a course.

> **Important:** the pillars are the **end state**, not the launch state. Season 1 deliberately
> runs without two of them. That is sequenced, not compromised, and the gaps are published. See
> [`14 — The Seasons Model`](./14-the-seasons-model.md).

### Pillar 1 — Someone is teaching it
Every programme has something that actually has the craft, transmitting it. Not a playlist, not a
PDF, not "content."

At Onliversity that is an **AI coach** — arrived in Season 2, built from what Season 1's players
learned. It does the research, watches your matches, and works with you day to day.

*Minimum bar:* every student can point to something that is accountable for their improvement.

*Season 1 status:* **absent, by design.** Season 1 is solo.

### Pillar 2 — People are learning with them
No enrolment into a void. Students join a cohort — a small group, moving at the same pace, with
each other's work in front of them.

*Minimum bar:* every student is in a group small enough that their absence would be noticed.

*Season 1 status:* present. It carries more weight in Season 1, because there is no coach carrying
the room.

### Pillar 3 — There is a cert after
Every programme ends. It has a finish line, a review, and something issued at the end that can be
shown to a parent, a client or an employer.

*Minimum bar:* nobody drifts out of Onliversity with nothing to show for the time.

*Season 1 status:* **the certificate does not exist yet.** It unlocks only once Onliversity has
helped at least ten people become professionals and win certificates elsewhere. Until then, Season
1 produces a **record** — matches, results, rank progression, VODs, lessons — and that record is
what a player leaves with. See [`14`](./14-the-seasons-model.md), section 4.

### Pillar 4 — There is a structure
Levels, expectations, entry points, progression, a calendar. It is a thing you *do*, on a
schedule, with other people — not a thing you *have*.

*Minimum bar:* a student can explain what level they are on and what comes next.

*Season 1 status:* present. This is what Season 1 actually is.

### The pillars, sequenced

| | Season 1 | Season 2 | At ten pros |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Someone teaching | ❌ solo by design | ✅ AI coach | ✅ |
| 2 — Learning with others | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| 3 — A credential | ❌ record instead | ❌ | ✅ certificate |
| 4 — A structure | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |

**The rule that keeps this honest:** every gap has to be visible to the student before they join,
and each one has to have a named season attached to it. A gap with a date on it is a roadmap. A gap
without one is a lie.

## 3. Minimum viable structure (Season 1)

What must exist before we enrol a single player — revised for a season with no coach and no
certificate:

- [x] **One discipline** — esports, EAFC 27
- [ ] **One curriculum** — a defined set of levels with a finish line
- [ ] **One cohort** — capped, named, enrolled together
- [ ] **One assessment route** — the ladder, VOD review and the accumulated record
- [ ] **One record** — public, per player, and explicitly *not* called a certificate
- [ ] **One community space** — where the cohort actually talks, daily
- [ ] **One transparency channel** — where decisions get published to the students
- [ ] **One public counter** — the ten-pro gate, visible to everyone
- [ ] **One lesson-capture mechanism** — Season 1's lessons are Season 2's training data, so this
      is infrastructure, not a nice-to-have

**No longer on the list:** a named mentor and a credential. Both are sequenced into later seasons
and both are declared publicly before anyone enrolls.

## 4. The first discipline: esports

The first product is **Preseason Academy** — esports, starting with EAFC 27. The four pillars map
onto it cleanly, because esports already invented its own version of them:

| Pillar | In esports at Onliversity | When |
|---|---|---|
| Someone is teaching it | An **AI coach** — does the research, watches your matches, works with you day to day | **Season 2** |
| People are learning with them | A **squad / cohort** that trains, scrimmages and reviews together | Season 1 |
| There is a cert after | A **public record** first — matches, results, rank, VODs. The **certificate** only at ten pros. | Record: Season 1 · Cert: ten pros |
| There is a structure | A **season**: preseason, competition, finish line. Levels by performance, not age. | Season 1 |

Full treatment in [`11 — Esports and the First Product`](./11-esports-and-the-first-product.md) and
[`14 — The Seasons Model`](./14-the-seasons-model.md).

## 5. What we refuse to fake

This section matters more than the parity table. A fake credential does more damage to this
mission than no credential at all — because the whole project is a claim about seriousness, and
one caught lie ends it.

| We will NOT claim | What we say instead |
|---|---|
| That there is a certificate in Season 1 | "There isn't one yet. It unlocks when ten of us make it as pros. Here's the record instead, and here's the counter." |
| That there is a coach in Season 1 | "Season 1 is solo — that's the design. The AI coach arrives in Season 2." |
| That the certificate is a degree | "It is an Onliversity certificate, and here is the record that backs it." |
| That we are accredited | "We are not accredited yet. Here is what we are, and here is the path." |
| That we replace school | "We run alongside it. This is not an alternative to school." |
| That this is a shortcut | "This is a real programme with real work in it." |
| Scale we don't have | The real number. Every time. |

The naming question — what we call the credential and what we are legally allowed to call
ourselves — is logged in [`06-open-questions.md`](./06-open-questions.md) as the first thing to
settle.
