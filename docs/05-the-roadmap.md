# 05 — The Roadmap

Online now. Physical eventually, inshallah.

The order below is strict. Each stage earns the next one. The gates between stages are the whole
discipline — they are what stops this from becoming a building with nothing inside it.

---

## Stage 0 — Foundation

*Prove the structure can be described before proving it can be run.*

- [ ] The four pillars defined and agreed (doc 02)
- [ ] First discipline chosen
- [ ] First curriculum written end to end, with a finish line
- [ ] **No mentor and no certificate** — both are sequenced, see [`14 — The Seasons Model`](./14-the-seasons-model.md)
- [ ] The public record designed — and the naming question settled (doc 06)
- [ ] The lesson-capture mechanism designed — Season 1's output is Season 2's input
- [ ] Transparency channel live

**Gate:** a stranger can read the curriculum and tell you exactly what a student does, week by
week, and what they hold at the end.

## Stage 1 — First cohort

*Prove the structure runs.*

- [ ] One cohort, capped, enrolled together
- [ ] Real teaching happening on a schedule
- [ ] A live community space the cohort actually uses daily
- [ ] First critiques held
- [ ] First portfolios shipped
- [ ] First public records closed out at the end of the season

**Gate:** students complete, and every one of them leaves with public work. Nobody drifts out with
nothing.

## Stage 2 — Proof

*Prove it repeats.*

- [ ] Second and third cohorts run without the founder doing everything
- [ ] Completion and retention numbers published openly
- [ ] **The AI coach built** from Season 1's captured lessons — pillar 1 arrives here
- [ ] More disciplines
- [ ] Student voices shaping the curriculum — visibly, with credit
- [ ] First graduates with paid work or clients, documented

**Gate:** the outcomes are real and published, and the programme survives without being carried by
one person.

## Stage 3 — Scale across Africa

*Prove it is continental, not local.*

- [ ] Multiple countries and states reached
- [ ] The AI coach serving players across the continent
- [ ] Community organised regionally, not just per cohort
- [ ] Recognition pursued — partnerships, and accreditation where it is available
- [ ] **Ten pros reached** — the certificate unlocks, and it is the first credential we have earned
  rather than printed
- [ ] Enrolment numbers published continuously

**Gate:** Onliversity is recognisably big in Africa. This is the stage that moves the parents —
not because we told them anything, but because it became a fact they could see.

## Stage 4 — Physical, inshallah

*The structure becomes a place.*

- [ ] Location chosen
- [ ] Studios built as the centre of the campus, not an annex
- [ ] Hybrid model: the online structure keeps running alongside
- [ ] The students who built it involved in building it

**Gate:** there is no gate after this one. This is the destination.

---

## What we will not do

- **Not build the campus early.** It is the reward for the structure working, not the way to make
  it work.
- **Not launch ten disciplines at once.** Depth first. One discipline done properly beats six done
  thinly, and the first cohort's portfolios are our most valuable artefact.
- **Not enrol students before the curriculum and the ladder exist.** That would break pillar 4 on
  day one — and pillar 4 is the only pillar Season 1 has to carry.
- **Not chase enrolment numbers at the cost of completion.** A large intake with a collapse at the
  end is the worst possible evidence, and we would have published it ourselves.
- **Not hide a failed stage.** If a stage does not pass its gate, that gets written down and
  published like everything else.
