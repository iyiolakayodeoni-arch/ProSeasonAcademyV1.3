## On the brand

I will need to feel seen and heard — like it needs to feel Gen Z and African. That's what will
convince them. I have done this already for the video; it addresses the issue of how most parents
shut down anything that is not science. Purple and black. Something different, and the branding
should have memes and should feel like it is actually for teenagers and young adults with the
current trends.

I am thinking of a guy that is there — it should be like, I would be there as the founder, not my
face though. Like an animated character that looks like me in a way. He would be there with a bunch
of people asking questions, then they would see me answering questions about Onliversity — all the
questions that most teenagers might have.

## On the video

That's what it would be, but it needs to be an actual continuation of the current video — the plot
needs to be completed. I might actually have — instead of ending it there, let's do it like this:
let's actually cut into my pitch, which is me there as the founder of Onliversity talking about how
we can help the person there and what our product does, but in form of an actual press conference
talking about everything we do — but the parts they care about first, sha.

Or I can say something like "do you sometimes" — or instead of doing that, let it transition in a
way that the earlier video was actually a video in a pitching and publicity stunt for Onliversity.
Yes, exactly. So it would now be like we are talking to investors and people alike.

So it would be saying something like:

> Do you sometimes feel like the young man in this video — like your talents and creativity are
> always seen as the backup plan, and not the main career or main thing? Well, I have felt the same
> way as well, which is why I am building Onliversity — the digital university for creatives. We
> are starting with esports to give structure to esports players, and the first game we are
> starting with is EAFC 27. We are building this product, Preseason Academy, as the first product
> from Onliversity.

I think it should actually continue from that story of the guy feeling dejected, then it goes to me
the founder saying something like:

> Omo, Mumsi dey actually vex oooo. My people, I actually have a startup product that solves this
> woman's fears. She is afraid that her son's future is ruined if he doesn't go to uni — and she is
> right, to be honest. With the way the country is, uni increases your chance of getting a good job
> and actually doing something for yourself.
>
> I am not here to sell a dream to you. I am here to provide an alternative. Wouldn't you like to
> live in a world where your creative passion no longer becomes a passion, but is a real career
> that is respected, and people do not bang the door on your accomplishment? That is the problem my
> startup solves. The name is Onliversity — it is a digital university for creatives, giving you
> the structure that people mock us for lacking.
>
> This sounds all good and fine, but I know what you are thinking: another startup, another
> subscription that I didn't even plan for. Relax — you get to use the product for one week, which
> is called the Founders Week, where you use it and get to decide what the price is, in real time.
> So the price is free and fair, and not hardcoded by the organisation — us. Or Claude. Am I saying
> us? Like, say no be me and Claude build this thing.
>
> So this is open source — not in the sense that you can fork features, more of you get to decide
> the new features that are being added in the application. So it's built by the people, in a way.
>
> Enough of my yapping. Go to Onliversity.app — there you can get more information on everything
> you need to know, the details of the product, everything is there. I am not saying the product
> details because I know all of you — nah, the price be the problem, no be the value. Hand dey meet
> everybody, I know. We understand that, and we have put that into consideration. So don't forget —
> click the link and sign up. Let's build this university for creatives together.
