Onliversity. I want this to be a place where a child that is like me can actually feel like they
matter and their dreams are valid — giving them the structure that universities have.

So the question now is this: what structure do those universities have that makes them work well?
They actually have a community behind them. Students — people are there learning with them.
There is a cert after. There is someone teaching it.

From my thinking this all boils down to the belief that creative careers are not important. So I
need to show the value that we bring without taking away the sciences.

I can't change their minds without evidence, especially the parents. Their minds would be changed
once they see how big Onliversity gets in Africa. That's the only way to change their mind —
nothing I say would.

But what I can do to convince them is show that we have the same structure that a university would
have, but better, and way more suited for creative careers. The children need this. I'm not really
convincing them as much, so this is my selling point: we have value as creatives and we can build a
structure.

This would be ours — our own uni, that would eventually become physical, inshallah.

So this would be our project, our uni. They are involved in the process. No hiding things from
them. Everything is transparent.
