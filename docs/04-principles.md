# 04 — Principles

Seven things Onliversity does not trade away. When a decision is unclear, it gets settled here.

---

## 1. Nothing is hidden from the students

They are involved in the process. Everything is transparent — the decisions, the numbers, the
mistakes, the things we do not know yet.

This is not a courtesy. It is the only way the promise survives contact with reality. We are
asking a child to trust a structure that is still being built in front of them. If we build it
behind a closed door and then present it, we have proven that they did not matter after all.

**In practice:** decisions get published with the reasoning attached. Metrics get published
whether they are good or bad. When we do not know something, we say we do not know it. When we get
something wrong, that gets published too.

## 2. The students are co-builders, not customers

This is our uni. Ours — theirs.

A customer is served. A co-builder has a stake, a voice, and a record of what they helped make.
The difference shows up in whether students get to shape the curriculum, name things, review the
work of the programme itself, and be credited for what they built.

If the students are only consuming, we have built a course. If they are building, we have built a
university.

## 3. Structure is how we say "you matter"

The entire promise of this place is that the child matters and the dream is valid. Words do not
deliver that — a child has already heard the words and correctly discounted them.

Structure delivers it. A cohort that notices when you are missing. A ladder that shows exactly
where you stand. A level you have actually earned. A record of every match you played. Those are
not administrative details; they are the message itself, in a form that cannot be dismissed.

**Corollary:** the structure must be real. A hollow version of this — a certificate with nothing
behind it — is worse than nothing, because it teaches the child that even their version of
seriousness is pretend. **That is exactly why there is no certificate in Season 1.** It unlocks
only when ten of our players have made it as professionals. See
[`14 — The Seasons Model`](./14-the-seasons-model.md).

## 4. Complement, never compete, with the sciences

We are not against the sciences and we are not asking any child to choose. We are saying that
creative work also produces real value, and that it also deserves structure.

This is both true and strategically correct. We do not need anyone to value physics less. We need
them to see what happens when a creative child is finally given a path.

**Corollary:** any messaging that scores a point against the sciences gets cut, however good the
line is.

## 5. We show, we do not argue

We have already conceded the central fact: no argument changes a parent's mind. Evidence does.

So the reflex in every situation is to produce an artefact rather than make a claim — a portfolio
rather than a promise, a number rather than an assertion, a recorded match rather than a
description of progress.

**Corollary:** we never inflate a number, and we publish the bad ones. The credibility of the good
numbers depends entirely on the bad ones being real.

**Corollary:** we do not spend effort arguing with parents at all. The audience is the teenager;
the parent moves on their own once the scale does. See
[`03 — The Evidence Strategy`](./03-the-evidence-strategy.md), section 1.

## 6. We do not claim what we have not built

No fake credentials. No implied accreditation. No borrowed prestige. Where we fall short of what a
university has, we say so plainly and put it on the roadmap.

This is uncomfortable and it is the point. The whole project is a claim about seriousness, and the
fastest way to destroy that claim is to be caught overstating.

See *What we refuse to fake* in [`02 — The University Blueprint`](./02-the-university-blueprint.md).

## 7. Physical is the destination, proven-online is the road

The campus is the goal — inshallah. It is also the final piece of evidence, the thing that makes
the project impossible to dismiss.

But it comes after. The structure gets built online, proven with real cohorts, and scaled across
Africa first. A building erected before the structure works is a shell with nothing inside it.

**Corollary:** every decision gets tested against "does this make the online structure more real,
or is it decoration?"
