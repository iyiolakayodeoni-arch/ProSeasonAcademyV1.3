# Onliversity

A university structure for creative kids in Africa — so that the ones who were told their dreams
don't count get the same scaffolding everybody else got.

Online now. Physical eventually, inshallah.

---

## The idea, in four lines

1. A creative child learns early that their dream does not count. Not because it lacks value —
   because nobody built a structure for it.
2. Universities work because they have four things: **someone teaching**, **people learning
   alongside you**, **a curriculum with levels**, and **a credential at the end**. We are building
   all four — but in seasons, and we will not issue a credential we have not earned.
3. Give creative kids exactly that structure, adapted for how creative work is actually learned.
4. Do not argue with anyone about whether creative careers matter. Build it, publish everything,
   and let the evidence do the work.

## The selling point

> **The same structure a university has — but better, and way more suited to creative careers.**

"The same structure" is the credibility claim: we are not asking anyone to imagine a new category,
we are pointing at one they already respect. "Better suited" is the differentiator — better *for
this child*, not better than university in the abstract.

## The non-negotiables

1. Nothing is hidden from the students.
2. The students are co-builders, not customers.
3. Structure is how we say "you matter."
4. Complement the sciences, never compete with them.
5. We show, we do not argue.
6. We do not claim what we have not built.
7. Physical is the destination; proven-online is the road.

Details in [docs/04-principles.md](./docs/04-principles.md).

## Who we are talking to

**The teenager.** Not the parent. Convincing parents is a waste of time — the argument is
unwinnable, and unnecessary, because Onliversity getting big across Africa moves them without us
asking. The parent is a byproduct, not a target.

## What we are building first

**Preseason Academy** — the first product from Onliversity. Esports, starting with **EAFC 27**.

It runs in **seasons**, and the structure arrives in stages:

| | Season 1 | Season 2 | At ten pros |
|---|---|---|---|
| Structure and community | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Someone teaching | ❌ **solo by design** | ✅ AI coach | ✅ |
| A credential | ❌ a record instead | ❌ | ✅ the certificate |

**Season 1 is a solo journey** — become the best you can be on your own, and the lessons you
produce become what the Season 2 AI coach is built from. **There is no certificate yet.** It
unlocks only once Onliversity has helped at least ten people become professionals and win
certificates elsewhere.

Both gaps are published, not hidden. Details in
[docs/14-the-seasons-model.md](./docs/14-the-seasons-model.md).
