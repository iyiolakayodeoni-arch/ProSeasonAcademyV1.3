# ProSeasonAcademy — founder brief

## Say this when someone asks what you built

**“ProSeasonAcademy is a private EA FC console review practice. A player plays a match, reviews the decisions that mattered in their own words, and carries one lesson into the next match.”**

That is the product. Everything else is supporting equipment.

## The problem it solves

Most players play a lot of FC but learn very little from the matches they lose. They remember the score, blame the game, watch a clip, or move to the next match. They do not build a record of the decisions, emotions, and patterns that repeat.

ProSeasonAcademy gives them a simple repeatable practice:

1. Play a real match.
2. Notice what happened.
3. Write one honest lesson.
4. Test that lesson next match.

Over time, the player gets evidence of what actually improves—not a streak of motivational content.

## What a member should understand in their first minute

| Question | Plain answer |
|---|---|
| What do I do here? | Start a Match Review around your next match. |
| What is a Match Review? | A short before/during/after-match review in your own words. |
| What is Your Lesson? | The one lesson you carry from the last match. |
| What is Match History? | Your saved scores and notes. |
| What is Progress? | Six chapters that move only when your evidence earns them. |
| Do I have to follow news or chat? | No. Those are optional support tools. |

## The five-second test

A first-time player should be able to answer these questions without scrolling or learning a branded term:

| Question | The interface must answer |
|---|---|
| What is this? | A way to learn from my FC match. |
| What do I tap? | **Start my match review**. |
| What if my match has not started? | Choose **I’m about to play**. |
| What if it already ended? | Choose **I already finished a match**. |
| What if I have no match today? | Leave the app; come back after a real match. Nothing is overdue. |

The Home screen should always preserve one obvious green action above explanations, statistics, coach lore, news, and community.

## The hierarchy to protect

### 1. Core product — never bury this
**Play → Review → Carry**

- **Today:** tells the player their next useful action.
- **Match Review:** captures the review.
- **Your Lesson:** carries the lesson.
- **Progress:** makes the long-term evidence visible.

### 2. Supporting evidence
- Match History
- Loss Notes
- Starting Week

### 3. Optional utilities
- Founder updates / FC news
- Community
- Advanced seven-match statistics tracker

If a utility competes visually with the core product, it belongs behind a secondary link.

## What the app is not

Do not position it as:

- a generic FC tips feed;
- a social network for gamers;
- a live-stat or player-card generator;
- AI coaching that tells a player what they think;
- a shortcut to becoming a pro.

It is a disciplined reflection practice for serious console players.

## The feature filter

Before approving any future feature, ask:

> Does this directly help a player **play, review, or carry one lesson forward**?

- **Yes:** it may belong in the main member experience.
- **Maybe:** it belongs under Progress or a secondary utility.
- **No:** it should stay outside the player app.

## The product language to use

Use the plain player language everywhere in the member app.

| Use | Do not make a new player learn |
|---|---|
| **Match Review** | “Mirror Session” |
| **Your Lesson** | “The Thread” |
| **Match History** | “Match Vault” |
| **What Good Looks Like** | “The Standard” |
| **Progress chapter** | “Stage progression” |
| **Optional Tip** | “Side Quest” |
| **Community** | “The Halls” |

Brand language belongs in founder material or coach flavour only. It should never be required to understand the next action.
